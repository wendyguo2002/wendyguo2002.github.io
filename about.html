About Me

I'm a Computer Science master's student at UC San Diego and a researcher in the Center for Wireless Communications. My work combines physically based simulation, machine learning, and sensing to create synthetic data that stays grounded in how the world behaves.

I am especially interested in the boundary between graphics and RF: how motion, geometry, and material properties become sensor observations, and how differentiable simulators can help models learn from the cases they find difficult.
