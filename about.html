About Me

I’m a master’s student at UC San Diego working on physically based simulation, synthetic data, and machine learning for sensing.

My current work combines controllable motion generation, differentiable RF simulation, and real radar measurements. I also build interactive tools that make simulation workflows easier to author, inspect, and reproduce.
